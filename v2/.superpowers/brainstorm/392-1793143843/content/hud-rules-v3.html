1 · Surfaces: none

Text never sits in a box. One shadow on all text + a corner scrim for contrast.

2 · Objects: one style

Key caps, minimap, bars: blue 2px stroke, slanted corners, dark fill. Nothing else gets a stroke.

3 · Spacing: 8 / 24 / 48

8 inside a group, 24 between groups, 48 to the screen edge. Every group hangs off a corner; right corners right-align.

4 · Type: 2 styles × 5 sizes

Title (SemiBold) for values, Main (Medium) for words. Labels are Main 16 dim. Sizes 16 · 20 · 28 · 48 · 80.

5 · Color: white + blue

Blue = you / your progress. Gold reward, red danger, green money. Nothing else.

6 · Motion: three verbs

Pop a value went up (score 30). Wheelie a callout arrives (tilts back from bottom-left, like the logo's o). Punch a tier changed (×2). New feed lines slide + flash. Shake reserved for danger.
