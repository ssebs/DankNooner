# Story

## Rise to Glory / Zero to Hero / Stank to Dank

Simpsons Hit & Run style: each chapter moves you across one world and comes with its own bike.
The systems side and the world layout are in [Progression](./Progression.md).

- Be the best racer in town 😎
- Start on crappy races, then do harder ones
  - Need to do tricks to get NOS, needed to win
- Get golds / trophies
- NPC quests with basic Animal Crossing style dialog

### Chapter 1: Bad side (mini bike)

- Dark, dingy and older, with few neon lights. Across the railroad tracks from the city.
- Learn tricks; grom parking-lot races with cones
- Save up $ to buy the naked bike for the area's final race

Candidate missions from the old bicycle prologue:

- Start the game with a cutscene, leading into gameplay; ride around, do crappy wheelies
- Deliver newspapers
- See a group of others stunting on motorcycles, and go watch (cutscene)
- Learn how to do proper wheelies
- Stunt battle at the skatepark
- Street race

### Chapter 2: Big city (naked bike)

- The nicer part of the city, lots of neon
- More quests, stunt races, time trials, stunt battles
- Cross the railroad tracks to the interstate for longer, faster races
- Unlock the sport bike and more customization

### Chapter 3: Calm side (ending)

- Today's stunt track 01: a blend of everything. Neon, but also a quiet, dim, relaxing beach town
  with farms, Monterey / Cali vibe.
- The island race track
- Dirt bike on the beach

### Classic mode (outside the story)

- Road trip: a touring bike along the highway across states, Iron Butt style
- Endless loop like the original game
- Unlock the cruiser if you want
